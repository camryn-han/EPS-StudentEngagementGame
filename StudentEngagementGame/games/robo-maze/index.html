How to play
  1. Add movement actions with the arrows (or keyboard ↑ ↓ ← → / WASD).
  2. Press Run (or Enter) and the robot walks your program.
  3. 🔒 tiles block the way. Move next to one and click it, or walk into it, to get a question.
  4. Answer correctly and the tile opens. Then press Run again to continue.
  5. Reach 🏁 to finish the level.
